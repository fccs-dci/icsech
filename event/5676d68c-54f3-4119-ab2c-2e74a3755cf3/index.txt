Before the fourteenth century,&nbsp;<em>guoshi</em>&nbsp;referred primarily to Buddhist and Daoist court preceptors. In Ming local writings, however, the title was retrospectively applied to geomancers active between the ninth and eleventh centuries in southern mountain communities, recasting them as authoritative masters of terrain-based fengshui.</p>
