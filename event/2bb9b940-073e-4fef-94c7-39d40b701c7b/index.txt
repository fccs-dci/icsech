13:T6db,<p>In <em>Cruisy, Sleepy, Melancholy: Sexual Disorientation in the Films of Tsai Ming-liang</em>&nbsp;(University of Minnesota Press, 2022), Nicholas de Villiers contends that we need to theorize both queer time and space to understand Taiwan-based director Tsai Ming-liang's cinematic explorations of feeling melancholy, cruisy, and sleepy.&nbsp;Building on those arguments, this presentation starts with a reading of Tsai’s short film&nbsp;It’s a Dream&nbsp;(2007)—set in a movie theater in Malaysia—as a microcosm of Tsai’s themes and motifs of sleep/dreaming, cruising, nostalgia, and the space of the cinema.
