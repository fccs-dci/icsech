Rice cultivation, alongside the utilization of various starchy plants, has persisted over millennia since the early Neolithic. This study examines plant impressions found in pottery sherds dating to the early Shangshan period (ca. 12,711–8,538 cal bp) to shed light on the significance of barnyard grass (Echinochloa sp.) during this era.
